## Services

Lagos Dental Centre offers:

- Preventive dentistry
- Tooth restoration
- Clear aligners
- Cosmetic dentistry
- Crowns and bridges
- Dental implants (placed and restored in-house; single tooth through full-arch)
- Dentures
- Emergency dentistry (same-day priority for patients in pain)
- Extractions / extractions teeth care
- Sedation

### Highlighted offerings

**Dental implants**  
Permanent, natural-feeling replacement for missing teeth; planning through long-term maintenance handled in-house.

**Emergency dentistry**  
Same-day appointments prioritized for patients in pain; call the office to be assessed quickly.

**Clear aligners**  
Aligner therapy with attention to bite and overall oral health, not only tooth position.

**Zen Suite**  
A calm operatory designed for patients with dental anxiety and neurodivergent needs — warm tones and a deliberate comfort-first environment.
